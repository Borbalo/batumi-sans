Batumi Sans Rounded is the rounded companion to Batumi Sans, a type family for Georgian, Latin, Cyrillic and Greek designed for Borbalo by Koba Jgenti and derived from FiraGO, Fira Sans and Noto Sans Georgian. It has round stroke ends, variable weight (wght 100–900) and width (wdth 75–115), true cursive italics, and all four Georgian scripts.

To contribute, see github.com/Borbalo/batumi-sans
