Batumi Sans is a type family for Georgian and Latin, designed for Borbalo by Koba Jgenti and derived from FiraGO, Fira Sans and Noto Sans Georgian. It has variable weight (wght 100–900) and width (wdth 75–115), true cursive italics, all four Georgian scripts, and a Georgian ampersand made from დ and ა, alongside Latin, Cyrillic and Greek.

To contribute, see github.com/Borbalo/batumi-sans
